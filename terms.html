Terms of service

Last updated 23 October 2026

By using BorlaAlert GH you agree to these terms. The platform connects residents with independent collectors for on-demand waste pickup in Greater Accra.

Booking fee

The GH₵ 2.00 fee is a platform booking charge paid by Mobile Money. Volume carting at the gate is agreed and settled between the resident and the collector.

Acceptable use

Do not request pickup of hazardous, medical, or illegal waste. Collectors must not dump roadside. Missed gates, unsafe access, or false documents may lead to account suspension.

Collectors

Collectors must complete Ghana Card and vehicle checks before going on duty. Earnings are paid to the MoMo wallet on file after completed jobs, minus any stated platform share.

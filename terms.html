Terms of Service

By accessing or using BorlaAlert GH, you agree to these Terms of Service. BorlaAlert GH provides a software coordination platform connecting households in Greater Accra with independent motorized tricycle (Aboboyaa) waste collectors.

Transparent Two-Tier Pricing: The GH₵ 2.00 fee charged through Mobile Money is strictly a software booking and coordination fee for BorlaAlert GH. Physical waste carting and disposal (typically GH₵ 15 – 25) is negotiated and paid directly to your independent collector at the gate.

payments 1. Platform Fees and Gate Carting

BorlaAlert GH facilitates on-demand collection requests by matching residents with vetted local collectors:

  • Software Booking Fee: A flat GH₵ 2.00 fee is authorized by the resident via Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, or AT Money) to initiate the dispatch request.
  • Gate Carting Fee: Carting fees are based on waste volume, number of bins or bags, and weight. Residents pay the collector directly at their gate via cash or direct personal MoMo. BorlaAlert GH does not take a commission from this carting fee.
  • Refund Policy: If no local collector accepts your booking within 15 minutes, or if a collector cancels prior to arrival, the GH₵ 2.00 platform fee is automatically reversed to your wallet.

security 2. Waste Guidelines and Acceptable Use

To safeguard community health, environment, and collector safety, residents agree to abide by municipal sanitation regulations:

  • Acceptable Waste: Bagged household refuse, clean sorted plastics (sachets, HDPE bottles), scrap metals, organic/yard waste, and dry packaging.
  • Strictly Prohibited: Hazardous materials, medical/clinical waste, car batteries, flammable chemicals, toxic industrial waste, and uncontained sharp objects.
  • Illegal Dumping: Collectors registered on BorlaAlert GH are strictly prohibited from dumping waste roadside, into drainage gutters, or unauthorized public areas. Waste must be carted to designated Accra Metropolitan Assembly (AMA) transfer stations or EPA-licensed recycling drop-offs.

badge 3. Independent Collector Obligations

Collectors on BorlaAlert GH operate as independent waste service providers:

  • Collectors must undergo Ghana Card verification and provide valid vehicle registration details (Aboboyaa motorized tricycles or light trucks).
  • Collectors must maintain courteous conduct, arrive at the confirmed GhanaPost GPS location, and issue transparent carting quotes based on standard community rates.
  • Failure to complete accepted pickups or violations of EPA environmental disposal guidelines will lead to immediate account suspension and notification to municipal environmental health officers.

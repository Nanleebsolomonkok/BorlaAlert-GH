Privacy Policy

BorlaAlert GH values your trust and is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). We collect only what is strictly necessary to arrange waste pickups, coordinate dispatch, and protect platform safety.

Data Protection Commitment: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Household addresses and GPS locations are shared solely with the assigned collector during active collection windows.

storage 1. Information We Collect

To provide reliable waste collection coordination across Greater Accra, we collect:

  • Contact Information: Phone number used for SMS one-time passcode (OTP) verification and pickup dispatch alerts.
  • Location Data: GhanaPost GPS digital address code, street name, neighborhood landmarks, and temporary GPS coordinates during active pickup navigation.
  • Payment References: Mobile Money transaction reference IDs for auditing the GH₵ 2.00 software booking fee. We do not store or process your personal Mobile Money PIN.
  • Collector Documentation: Ghana Card (NIA) number, driver licence, vehicle registration (DVLA), and operating district for identity verification.

share 2. How Information is Used & Shared

Your data is used strictly for operational purposes:

  • Matching your household with nearby independent collectors in your municipal district.
  • Providing real-time arrival estimates and directions to the assigned tricycle driver.
  • Crediting Eco-Points for verified plastic and recyclable sorting.
  • Aggregating anonymous municipal tonnage metrics for district assembly environmental reporting.

lock 3. Security & Data Retention

All data transmitted between your browser and our platform is encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption. Demonstration records in client storage (such as localStorage) remain strictly within your local browser sandbox and can be cleared at any time via browser settings.

manage_accounts 4. Your Privacy Rights & Inquiries

You have the right to request access to your personal information, request corrections, or request deletion of your account. For inquiries regarding data protection, please contact our privacy officer via Support or email privacy@borlaalert.gh.

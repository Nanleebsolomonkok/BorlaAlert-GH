Privacy

Last updated 23 October 2026

BorlaAlert GH collects only what is needed to arrange a pickup, pay the GH₵ 2.00 booking fee, and keep collectors and residents safe.

What we collect

  • Phone number, for sign-in and SMS codes
  • Pickup address, GhanaPost GPS, and live location while a job is active
  • MoMo wallet details needed to charge the booking fee
  • For collectors: Ghana Card, vehicle, and licence documents for vetting

How we use it

We use this information to match collectors, show arrival tracking, process MoMo payments, review collector eligibility, and respond to support requests. We do not sell personal data.

Your controls

You can update your address and wallet in Account. Collectors can request document removal after leaving the platform, subject to legal retention for payouts and disputes. Contact Support to ask about access or deletion.

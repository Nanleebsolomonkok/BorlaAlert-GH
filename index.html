timeline Straightforward Process

Four Steps to a Cleared Compound

Built specifically for Ghanaian households and informal waste collector logistics.

01

Select Waste Category

Open the web app, select your household waste type (regular rubbish, pure-water sachets, or yard waste), and confirm your gate address.

02

Authorize GH₵ 2.00 Fee

Approve the standard GH₵ 2.00 platform booking prompt on your phone using MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash, or AT Money.

03

Collector Dispatched

The dispatch engine routes your request to an available vetted Aboboyaa driver in your zone who accepts the assignment.

04

Pickup & Gate Settlement

The collector arrives at your compound gate, loads your waste, and you settle the carting fee directly with the driver.

verified_user Operating Principles

Built on Responsibility & Trust

How BorlaAlert GH establishes accountable sanitation in Ghanaian communities.

schedule

Predictable Scheduling

Eliminating the uncertainty of waiting indefinitely for informal collectors or chasing tricycles down neighborhood streets.

handshake

Fair Driver Compensation

Independent collectors keep 100% of their gate carting fees. The platform never takes deductions from the physical collection wage.

shield

Vetted Identities

Collector partners submit their Ghana Card National ID, vehicle documentation, and assembly permits during onboarding.

eco

Cleaner Gutters & Beaches

Rewarding household plastic segregation directly reduces the volume of pure-water sachets entering municipal drainage and coastal waters.

code Engineering & Research

Technology & Development Roadmap

Honest separation of current production capabilities and ongoing academic research.

Production Capabilities

Live Platform Services

PWA offline support, state persistence via localStorage, Mobile Money transaction webhooks (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money), digital trip manifests, and multi-role web consoles.

In Research & Evaluation

Algorithmic Dispatch Roadmap

Research prototypes in development: AI collector-to-gate assignment heuristics, zonal market day demand forecasting, and photo-based volume estimation using on-device machine learning models.

school
AI dispatch architecture and core platform engineering developed by Solomon Nanleeb, Computer Science & Engineering Department, University of Mines and Technology (UMaT), Tarkwa, Ghana.

GhanaPost GPS Digital Address

A GhanaPost GPS address gives your household a unique digital address code formatted as: GA-183-9021

How to find your digital address:
  • Check the blue digital plate affixed to your gate or wall.
  • Open the official GhanaPost GPS app on your phone.
  • Check any recent ECG or Ghana Water utility bill.

Note: This frontend prototype does not ping external geolocation services without your consent.

How Eco-Points Work

Eco-Points is a civic cleantech incentive designed to promote waste segregation at Ghanaian households. Every point you earn helps track your contribution to a cleaner Accra.

Ways to earn points:
  • +150 Points: Segregating pure water sachets into separate bags.
  • +200 Points: Clean PET bottles & beverage containers.
  • +50 Points: Prompt doorstep handoff to your collector.

Note: Eco-Points accumulate in your profile. Full redemption options launch with the live platform.

Frequently Asked Questions

help_outline What happens if my assigned collector does not arrive?

Open your Pickups tab in the app to view collector location. If a collector cannot reach your gate, you can cancel without penalty or request an instant refund of the GH₵ 2.00 software booking fee.

help_outline How do I pay the collector for waste carting?

The GH₵ 2.00 software booking fee covers dispatch coordination on the platform. The physical carting fee (typically GH₵ 15 – 25 based on bin size) is paid directly to the collector at your gate in cash or personal MoMo.

help_outline How do I register as an independent waste collector?

Visit the Collector Portal from the home page, enter your personal details, and upload your Ghana Card and Aboboyaa vehicle registration for verification by our municipal onboarding officers.
